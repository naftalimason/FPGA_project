Error episode #1 end to end (~2.665-2.700 ms) — the first injected parity
error travelling the whole resend flow: detect, count, {E} transmission,
flush, and the PC's resent frame.

At t = 2.677 ms the deliberately corrupted byte lands: phy_error pulses
(collect_en shows rx_mac had already started collecting the frame - the
partial hunt is flushed on the spot), rx_resend_ctrl's any_evt arms the
sticky resend_pending on the same tick, and uart_reg counts 0x000000 ->
0x000001. Crucially, tx_mac is still mid-reply at this moment (tx_busy high,
the fifo-status reply's last bytes 4C 7D still going out): the {E} has
priority only from IDLE, so the in-flight reply COMPLETES first - visible as
S_IDLE arriving at ~2.680 ms only after the 16th byte_done.

tx_mac then samples resend_pending: src_q flips to SRC_RESEND, len_q to 0x06,
and the byte rows spell the resend request on the wire - 7B, 45 ('E'), 00,
00, 00, 7D - eleven bit-times each, finishing at ~2.6885 ms where
resend_sent pulses. rx_resend_ctrl answers with the single-tick flush_rx
(fanned to both u_phy.flush_rx and u_mac.flush_rx): resend_pending clears,
the held/partial frame is dropped, and rx_phy enters its RESYNC re-arm.
After the guaranteed quiet gap, collect_en rises again at ~2.695 ms - the
TB's resent (clean) frame arriving - and is_msg re-asserts at ~2.700 ms:
one error, exactly one {E}, one count, full recovery.
