TX AHB read path (SRAM -> slave -> phy -> MAC -> tx_afifo), full 5 ms run —
the fill half of all three image-read sessions, and the fill-fast /
drain-slow rhythm imposed by the FIFO thresholds.

The read MAC leaves T_IDLE only inside the three S_BURST_RD windows
(~2.06-2.45 ms, ~3.3-3.55 ms, ~3.95-4.35 ms), each opened by the
tx_fifo_srst + start_read pair (after the settle guard). The phy_done /
accept rows expose the characteristic burst-read cadence: a DENSE cluster of
back-to-back SINGLE reads at each window start - the MAC races ahead at clk
speed until the three tx FIFOs hit almost_full (threshold 13) - followed by
sparse single reads every ~22.9 us, each one refilling the slot freed when
the consumer sends one payload on the UART. This is the early-throttle
policy (space_ok = !full & !almost_full) in action; full and err_overflow
never assert.

The data rows carry the readback proof. hrdata_o (the phy's only read-data
register) and the FIFO-bound wdata march through the stored image: session 1
ends on 0x000000FF (red word 15 of the original image), sessions 2 and 3 on
0xFFFFFF00 - its complement, confirming the inverted overwrite is what is
being streamed. raddr peaks at 0x000F (word 15) with the slave's
address-phase combinational raddr enabling the zero-wait read (rdata valid in
the data phase). Note HWRITE high only in the two WRITE windows: these are
the SAME three shared phys, handed between the two MACs by ahb_dir - during
the read windows this MAC owns them, during writes it sits in T_IDLE
watching its masked phy_done stay quiet.
