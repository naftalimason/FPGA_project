rx_afifo session flush at the start of image write #2 — the pointer reset,
its cross-domain lag, and the transient phantom flags safely contained inside
the v2.4 settle window.

At ~2,948,995 ns the accepted second IMG_WRITE_HDR moves the FSM S_IDLE ->
S_WR_SETUP: req_write and rx_fifo_srst pulse together, and img_status drops
0x41008 -> 0x01008 (req_write clears ready_to_read - the stored image is
being invalidated). The pointer rows then show the flush crossing domains:
wptr (gclk write side, raw srst) resets gray 0x18 -> 0x00 immediately, while
rptr (clk read side, srst via the toggle CDC) resets ~25 ns later - a
directly visible CDC lag between the two halves of the same logical flush.

In between, the write side's stale view produces the expected TRANSIENT
artefacts: full and almost_full pulse high for ~70 ns (zeroed write pointer
compared against the still-stale synced read gray 0x18) and almost_empty
dips briefly. This is the same class of race that caused BUG-1 on the tx
side - and the screenshot shows exactly why it is now harmless: the FSM
holds S_WR_SETUP for the whole settle count (~125 ns, until ~2,949,150 ns),
so the producer is still in P_IDLE while the phantoms live and die. Only at
S_BURST_WR does the producer arm (P_ARMED), with complete dropping as its
packed counter clears; img_rx_monitor -> 0x00000 and write_complete fall a
few ticks later (the cleared clk-side counter re-crossing the monitor sync).
Every flag is clean before the first payload can possibly arrive.
