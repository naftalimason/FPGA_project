The src_q / len_q pair segments the traffic precisely: SRC_REPLY frames with
len_q = 0x10 (register replies, including the recovery write/readback of
uart_checker - wdata 0x00BEEF11 and the checker register stepping 0xC3F0E1 ->
0xBEEF11 are visible mid-window) interleaved with SRC_RESEND episodes of
len_q = 0x06, where msg_q's top six bytes hold the constant
{7B 45 00 00 00 7D} (the lower bytes are stale reply data - only len_q bytes
are ever sent). Six {E} transmissions match the six injections.

The error-event rows and the uart_reg accumulator tell the counting story in
lock-step: phy_err_evt at 2.677 and 2.746 ms take uart_reg 0x000000 ->
0x000001 -> 0x000002; mac_err_evt at 2.767 ms makes it 0x000102; and the
three clsf_err_evt pulses at 2.788 / 2.816 / 2.851 ms walk the top byte
0x010102 -> 0x020102 -> 0x030102 - exactly {clsf=3, mac=1, phy=2}. At
~2.87 ms the TB's uart_reg read executes: the reply frame carries the
pre-clear value 0x030102 while the R0C clear commits on the same completed-
read cycle, and uart_reg snaps to 0x000000 - the second read visible at the
right edge returns zero, closing the read-to-clear verification. The addr
row parking at 0xFFFF between accesses and uart_config[0] = 1 (still ODD
parity here) complete the picture.
