TX MAC + PHY + uart_rgf, full 5 ms run — every FPGA->PC transmission of the
regression from the serialiser's point of view, with the error counters and
the runtime parity mode alongside.

The tx_mac serialiser is busy for most of the run (S_WAITB/P_DATA hash), with
clean S_IDLE/P_IDLE plateaus only where the DUT legitimately has nothing to
say - most prominently the T03 silent-refusal window (~1.3-1.6 ms). The
len_q row separates the two message families at a glance: 0x10 (16-byte
register replies and burst payloads) versus 0x06 - the six-byte {E} resend
requests, and msg_q correspondingly flips to the 0x7B45000000007D constant.
The 0x06 episodes line up exactly with the error-event rows below:
phy_err_evt at 2.677 / 2.746 / 3.922 ms, mac_err_evt at 2.767 ms, and
clsf_err_evt at 2.788 / 2.816 / 2.851 / 3.113 / 4.605 / 4.741 / 4.903 ms -
eleven injections, eleven {E} transmissions.

uart_rgf's rows document the bookkeeping: uart_reg accumulates the three
8-bit counters after each error cluster and snaps back to 0x000000 at every
R0C read (the reply carries the pre-clear value - visible as brief non-zero
windows). uart_config[0] shows the T08 runtime-parity window: it drops to 0
(EVEN) at ~3.83 ms and returns to 1 (ODD) at ~3.95 ms, bracketing the
even-mode traffic and the phy error injected inside it. uart_checker steps
through the test values (0x00C3F0E1 -> 0x00BEEF11 -> random-phase data), and
the envelope's addr row parks at 0xFFFF between APB accesses - the
apb_slave_envelope idling exactly per its spec.
