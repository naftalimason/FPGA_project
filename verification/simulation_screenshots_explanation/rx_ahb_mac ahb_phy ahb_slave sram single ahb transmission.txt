RX AHB write path, single-transaction anatomy — one complete FIFO-row-to-SRAM
write (row 5 of image write #1, data 0xFFFFFFFF) across all four layers in
~30 ns.

The trigger is the FIFO: the FWFT head rdata already shows 0xFFFFFFFF (the
memory read is combinational) and empty falls at ~1,524,543 ns when the
gclk-side push crosses the gray-pointer sync. With all three channels
non-empty (data_ok), the MAC leaves M_WAIT: xfer_req pulses one clk in
M_ISSUE at ~1,524,552 ns. The phy then executes the classic 2-phase AHB
SINGLE: S_ADDR drives HTRANS = 2 (NONSEQ) with HADDR stepping 0x00010 ->
0x00014, and one clk later S_DATA presents HWDATA while the zero-wait slave
(HREADY constant 1) commits: dphase_v/wr_d qualify u_slave.wen and u_sram.wen
for exactly one cycle, with the folded word address waddr = 0x0005
(HADDR[15:2]) and wdata = 0xFFFFFFFF.

Completion is a single-cycle chorus at ~1,524,570 ns: phy_done (S_DATA with
HREADY), the MAC's row_done pops all three FIFOs (pop_r/rd_en), haddr and the
monitor advance together 0x00014 -> 0x00018 (the counter IS the address), and
the FSM returns M_DONE -> M_WAIT. empty re-asserts ~2 clk later once the pop
crosses back through the sync, and rdata returns to the (not yet valid) next
slot. Total latency from data-visible to stored-in-SRAM is ~3 clk cycles -
against a 22,900 ns payload period, which is why the drain never falls behind
the UART and the FIFO depth never exceeds one row in steady state.
